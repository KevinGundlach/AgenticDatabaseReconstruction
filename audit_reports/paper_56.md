# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 56

## Scope

Compared eight records (Excel rows 698-705) with [Sweitzer, Shiflet, and Scully (2003)](<../papers/56_J.E. Sweitzer, G.J. Shiflet, J.R. Scully, Electrochim Acta, 48 (2003) 1223-1234.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A698:AM705】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

All eight pitting-potential means and confidence intervals agree with Table 1. One chemistry row is inconsistent: the crystalline Al90Fe5Gd5 record stores raw atomic percentages (Al 90/Fe 5/Gd 5) in wt% columns, whereas the same composition's other states are correctly converted to approximately Al 69.5/Fe 7.99/Gd 22.5 wt%. The treatment is 550 °C for 1 h, not 551 °C. The two pure-Al rows differ by electrode area, but that essential variable is not retained, leaving indistinguishable duplicates.

## Recommended corrections

Correct the crystalline chemistry, temperature typo, and electrode-area metadata; label all at%-to-wt% conversions and pH 7 as unreported.

## Overall assessment

**Potentials/coverage: pass. One composition row and key metadata: fail.**
