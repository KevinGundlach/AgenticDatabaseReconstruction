# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 74

## Scope

Compared eight records (Excel rows 556-563) with [Ahn, Kwon, and Lee (1998)](<../papers/74_Ahn, M. K., Hyuk-Sang Kwon, and HyuckMo Lee. Corrosion science 40.2-3 (1998)_ 307-322.pdf>). ## Findings

The base, Mo-only, and W-only 4 M MgCl2/80 °C Figure 3 values are plausible. For the base and low-W alloys the paper describes pitting at the corrosion potential, so the stored number is an onset/corrosion-potential proxy rather than a normally resolved Epit. Figure 5 adds three mixed Mo-W composition points that are omitted. Means come from 3-4 tests, but uncertainty is absent.

## Recommended corrections

Add the three mixed-alloy points, preserve the `pitting at Ec` endpoint flag, and attach replicate/graphical uncertainty. Keep `[Cl-]=8 M` because 4 M MgCl2 supplies 8 M chloride.

## Overall assessment

**Stored values: plausible. Coverage: incomplete. Endpoint metadata: needs correction.**
