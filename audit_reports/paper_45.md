# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 45

## Scope

Compared four records (Excel rows 150-153) with [Palit, Kain, and Gadiyar (1993)](<../papers/45_G. C. Palit, V. Kain , H. S. Gadiyaζ Corrosion 49 (1993)_ p. 977.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A150:AM153】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

The four source potentials—500 mV in 1 M HCl and 720/500/350 mV in 3/4/6.2 M NaCl—are correctly transcribed. The 1 M HCl record incorrectly stores `[Cl-]=0`; it must be 1 M. The 0.333 mV/s field is an equivalent of 20 mV per minute steps, not a continuous scan. NaCl pH 7 is not reported, and ambient temperature is left `NA`, appropriately.

## Recommended corrections

Set chloride to 1 M for the HCl row, describe the stepped protocol, and mark NaCl pH as unreported.

## Overall assessment

**Potentials: pass. HCl electrolyte metadata: fail. Coverage: pass.**
