# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 70

## Scope and limitation

Three Reference 70 records appear in Excel rows 68-70 for nitrogen-varied stainless steels in H2-deaerated 1 M H2SO4 + 0.5 M NaCl. Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A68:AM70】. The source PDF is missing. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Finding

The 265.5, 328.5, and 532.3 mV values and pH -0.301 cannot be independently verified. The pH is likely calculated from nominal acid concentration rather than measured.

## Recommended action

Mark all three rows source-unavailable/unverified and re-audit when the cited source is obtained.

## Overall assessment

**Unverified.**
