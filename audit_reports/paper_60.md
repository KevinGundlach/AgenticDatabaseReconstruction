## Findings

The four Table II values and 99% confidence intervals are correct: pure Al -376±113; Al90Fe5Gd5 amorphous -99±93, nanocrystalline +44±130, and crystalline -499±163 mV. The source heat treatments were performed in evacuated quartz tubes; Citrine incorrectly adds `H2O quenched` to the 150 °C/25 h and 550 °C/1 h conditions. Nominal at% chemistry was converted to wt% without labeling, while pH 5.9, 0.6 M NaCl, deaeration, 25 °C, and 0.1 mV/s are supported.

## Recommended correction

Remove the unsupported water-quench statements and label the chemistry conversion.
