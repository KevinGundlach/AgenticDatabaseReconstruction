# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 58

## Scope

Compared four records (Excel rows 204-207) with [Mischler et al. (1991)](<../papers/58_Mischler, S., et al. Corrosion science 32.9 (1991)_ 925-944.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A204:AM207】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Finding

Only Fe-24Cr in chloride-containing electrolyte B (0.12 M NaCl) pitted. Neither alloy pits in chloride-free electrolyte A, and Fe-24Cr-11Mo does not pit in B. Thus three of four database rows are transpassive/no-pitting values mislabeled as Epit. The source uses stepped potentials held about 400 s, not a continuous scan.

## Recommended correction

Retain only the Fe-24Cr/electrolyte-B pitting result, subject to reference-scale verification. Remove or censor the other three and correct the method description. Mark calculated pH as derived.

## Overall assessment

**Fail—three of four records have the wrong endpoint.**
