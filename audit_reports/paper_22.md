# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 22

## Scope

Compared six records (Excel rows 584-589) with [Lin and Tsai (2011)](<../papers/22_Chun-Ming Lin and Hsien-Lung Tsai. Intermetallics, 19 (2011), 288-294.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A584:AM589】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Executive summary

The six Table 4 potentials—130, 180, 280, 710, 140, and 180 mV—match the as-cast and five aged conditions. Composition values are derived from the nominal Al0.5CoCrFeNi atomic ratio rather than a measured wt% analysis, and the derivation is not identified. The paper's 304L comparison value (480 mV) is omitted.

| Severity | Finding |
|---|---|
| Medium | Nominal atomic ratios were converted to wt% and presented as measured exact chemistry. |
| Medium | Aged records omit the water-quench step. |
| Medium | 304L Epit = 480 mV is omitted. |
| Low | pH 7 is not source-reported. |

## Recommended corrections

Label chemistry as calculated nominal wt%; append water quenching to aged conditions; add the comparator if following the database's usual scope; and mark pH as unreported.

## Overall assessment

**Potentials: pass. Metadata/coverage: needs correction.**
