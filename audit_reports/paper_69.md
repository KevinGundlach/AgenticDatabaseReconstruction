# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 69

## Scope

Compared nine records (Excel rows 717-725) with [Moshier et al. (1987)](<../papers/69_W.C. Moshier, G.D. Davis, J.S. Ahearn, H.F. Hough, J Electrochem Soc, 134 (1987) 2677-2684.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A717:AM725】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

The nine Al-Mo Figure 6 values in deaerated 0.1 N KCl at pH 8 are plausible and the 0.2 mV/s scan is supported. Source compositions are atomic percent; Citrine converts them to wt% without recording that derivation. Temperature is not clearly reported for the electrochemical series, yet Citrine assigns 25 °C to most rows and 26 °C to pure Al, creating an unsupported inconsistency. Graphical uncertainty is absent.

## Recommended corrections

Set temperature to unreported unless source evidence is found, label at%-to-wt% conversion, and qualify the plotted readings.

## Overall assessment

**Potentials/coverage: pass with graphical caveat. Temperature metadata: fail.**
