# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 34

## Scope

Compared nine records (Excel rows 298-306) with [Lim et al. (2001)](<../papers/34_Y. S. Lim,]. S. Kim, 5.]. Ahn, H. S. Kwon, Y. Katada, Corros. Sci. 43 (2001)_p. 53.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A298:AM306】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

The four 316LN KCl values, four HCl values, and one high-Mn KCl value agree with the plotted endpoints within graphical uncertainty. Source compositions, SCE scale, 0.2 mV/s scan, and 25 °C condition are consistent. The graph-derived values are stored with excessive decimal precision. One KCl row has truncated method text, and pH 7 for KCl is assumed rather than reported; HCl pH is calculated.

## Recommended corrections

Round or qualify digitized values, restore the full method text consistently, and distinguish measured from calculated/assumed pH.

## Overall assessment

**Potentials/compositions: pass with precision and metadata caveats. Coverage: pass.**
