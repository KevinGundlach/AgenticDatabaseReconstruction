# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 14

## Scope

Reviewed the zero-row Reference 14 result against [Brigham and Tozer (1973)](<../papers/14_R. J. Brigham, E. W. Tozer, Corrosion 29 (1973)_ p. 33.pdf>) and the full `Pitting Potential` sheet. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Executive summary

Reference 14 is a confirmed coverage omission. Figures 1 and 2 directly plot the potential required to initiate pitting as a function of temperature for four alloys. Figure 1 alone contains roughly forty discrete markers. Figure 2 overlaps part of the same increasing-potential dataset and therefore requires de-duplication, but the source clearly contains primary pitting-potential observations.

| Severity | Finding |
|---|---|
| High | No database records exist despite extensive direct pitting-potential data in Figures 1-2. |
| Medium | The source endpoint is a specified initiation-current criterion; that proxy definition must be retained. |

## Recommended correction

Digitize the discrete markers, de-duplicate observations repeated across the two figures, record alloy identity and temperature, and preserve the paper's 10 µA/cm² initiation criterion and SCE reference. Markerless connecting lines should not be sampled as additional data.

## Overall assessment

**Coverage: fail—major omission.**
