# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 59

## Scope

Compared two records (Excel rows 208-209) with [Landolt et al. (1990)](<../papers/59_Landolt, D., et al.  Corrosion science 31 (1990)_ 431-440.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A208:AM209】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

The 487.5 mV value for Fe-24Cr in 0.12 M NaCl is a plausible pitting endpoint. The 884.3 mV value in chloride-free electrolyte A is transpassive behavior, not pitting; Citrine's own comment signals this uncertainty. The study uses long potentiostatic holds at selected potentials rather than a simple continuous scan.

## Recommended correction

Remove/reclassify the chloride-free row, retain the chloride-containing row with source endpoint provenance, and correct method/pH metadata.

## Overall assessment

**One record plausible; one false inclusion.**
