# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 64

## Scope

Compared two records (Excel rows 564-565) with [Ives et al. (1990)](<../papers/64_Ives, M. B., et al. Corrosion science 31 (1990)_ 367-376.pdf>). Workbook evidence: 【CRA_database_Scientific_Data_Publication_12102020.xlsx:Pitting Potential:A564:AM565】. Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Findings

The unimplanted and Mo-implanted sample readings, approximately 449 and 379 mV SCE, are plausible Figure 3 values. They are sample-specific graphical readings and the paper reports no reproducible implantation trend, so the pair must not be presented as a robust 70 mV causal effect. Surface implant dose is a treatment variable, not bulk Mo chemistry. Figure uncertainty and sample identity are insufficiently represented.

## Recommended correction

Label both values approximate, add sample/implant-dose provenance, and preserve the source's conclusion that no reproducible trend was established.

## Overall assessment

**Values: plausible. Interpretation/metadata: needs qualification.**
