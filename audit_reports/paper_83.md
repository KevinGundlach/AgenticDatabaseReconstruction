# Audit Report: Citrine Pitting-Potential Records for Reference 83

## Scope

Reviewed the zero-row result against [Sun et al. (2018)](<../papers/83_Y. T. Sun, J. M. Wang,Y. M. Jiang, J. Li, Materials and corrosion, 69 (2018) 44.pdf>). Audit date: 2026-09-04.

## Executive summary

Although the study centers on critical pitting temperature, Figures 3, 4, and 6-8 plot many discrete pitting/transpassive potentials versus temperature for 316L, 317L, 904L, and 254SMO in 1-3 M NaCl. No records exist. The paper supplies exact compositions, SCE reference, current-density criterion, and scan rates, but pitting markers must be separated from transpassive markers.

| Severity | Finding |
|---|---|
| High | Numerous direct pitting-potential markers in the Z-curve figures are omitted. |
| Medium | The source intentionally distinguishes pitting from transpassive/crevice behavior; that classification is essential during extraction. |

## Recommended correction

Extract only discrete pitting markers from the Z curves, record temperature, alloy, chloride concentration, scan rate, and the 100 µA/cm² endpoint. Do not sample connecting curves or label transpassive points as Epit.

## Overall assessment

**Coverage: fail for pitting-potential observations, even though CPT is the paper's principal endpoint.**
