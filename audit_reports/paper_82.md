## Finding

The paper emphasizes critical crevice temperature, LiCl concentration thresholds for whether pitting occurs, and microelectrochemical passivation parameters. Table 2 records pitting/no-pitting occurrence at 9-11 M LiCl rather than discrete Epit values. Acid microcell curves are explicitly evaluated by passivation potential/current because pitting-potential measurement is impractical at that scale. Zero ordinary Epit rows is therefore defensible.

## Recommended action

No pitting-potential correction is required. Preserve the LiCl occurrence thresholds for a future categorical/critical-concentration dataset.
