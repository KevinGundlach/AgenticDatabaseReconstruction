C1 - Conversion assumption for Nos. 332-349: interpret the paper's axis label "H2 scale" as the standard hydrogen scale. For comparison to a conventional 25 C saturated-KCl calomel electrode, use E(SCE vs NHE)=+0.2412 V from the [International Chemistry SCE specification](https://www.autolabj.com/construction.files/electrode.files/E-sce.htm); hence E_vs_SCE=(E_H2-0.2412) V. This is an external conversion constant, not a paper-reported calibration. The paper does not identify its reference-electrode implementation/temperature, and the CSV specifies only SCE. Numerical agreement is therefore conditional, particularly for the 0 C solution tests; test temperature does not establish reference temperature. The [Gamry SCE temperature calculator](https://www.gamry.com/resources-2/electrochemical-calculators-tools/saturated-calomel-electrode-calculator/) confirms temperature dependence. [Gamry's saturated-KCl product specification](https://orders.gamry.com/electrodes-and-accessories/saturated-calomel-reference-electrode.html) quotes 244 mV vs NHE; choosing that alternative room-temperature convention would lower the calculated values here by 2.8 mV. Neither convention was fitted to the targets. Plot coordinates/line thickness do not support the CSV's millivolt and thousandth-wt% precision. No composition-basis conversion is needed: the figure abscissae are wt%.

C2 - No. 339: Fig. 6 places the Mo-free 0 C point near 0.80 V (H2 scale), whereas the corresponding paragraph prints 0.78 V. The plotted value is the closer numerical candidate; both presentations refer to the same observation. Nos. 333-343 are Fig. 6 observations, and Nos. 344-349 are Fig. 8 observations. The "No Breakdown" extensions at high Mo and 0 C are not numerical pitting thresholds and were not assigned to target rows. Bromide tests were kept separate from chloride tests. Vc denotes the paper's long-time critical boundary (described on PDF p. 2), not an asserted mean. The separate non-steady V'c labels and continuous polarization curves were not substituted for Vc. All paper sections, figure panels, captions, tables and discussion were surveyed; no external experimental measurements or saved extraction outputs were used.
