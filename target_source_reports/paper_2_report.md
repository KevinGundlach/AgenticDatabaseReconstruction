N1 - Nos. 388-399: Table III footnote a establishes averages only for numerical pitting results; its NP entries are no-pitting observations up to the test ceiling. The approximately 0.8 V transpassive region is not a measured pit-onset value. Table I links the analyzed Mo contents to the nominal Table III rows; its NA means "not analyzed, but in the range of the series," not zero or absence of Cr/Ni. In particular, Nos. 389, 390 and 393-395 retain missing CSV Cr/Ni and cannot be independently identified from those missing fields; their Mo values, series placement and common HCl test support the proposed association. CSV Fe balances were not used as supporting evidence. The paper's "argon-saturated" and CSV "deaerated" refer to compatible oxygen-exclusion conditions; the CSV does not name the purge gas.

N2 - Nos. 400-403: use Table IV's stable-growth "Pitting potential" column, not its separate "Repassivating pitting potential" column. No. 402 preserves the two printed observations as CSV minimum/maximum; it is not assigned to a distinct unidentifiable replicate. No. 403 has a final-rate discrepancy (20 mV/hr, not 60); no source value was changed to repair it. The paper's general protocol on PDF p. 2 decreases the scan from 600 to 60 mV/hr near the expected pitting potential, so the single 0.167 mV/s CSV field for Nos. 388-399 does not describe their complete scan history. Rate conversion is mV/hr divided by 3600; the SCE reference is unchanged. No thresholds were inferred from unannotated polarization curves.
